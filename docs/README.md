# MouseShift documentation

<img src="images/logo.png" alt="MouseShift logo" width="96" align="right">

MouseShift adds an option missing from World of Warcraft's own key bindings: **a modifier on the mouse**. It turns the extra buttons on your mouse, or any key you choose, into Shift, Ctrl and Alt, and works alongside practically every other addon.

This documentation follows the addon from the first idea to everyday use.

1. **[Why MouseShift](why-mouseshift.md)**: the problem it solves, the ideas behind it and who it is for.
2. **[Getting started](getting-started.md)**: installing, assigning your buttons and planning your keybinds, with example layouts.
3. **[Healers](healers.md)**: mouseover healing with more keybinds than you have fingers.
4. **[MMO mice](mmo-mice.md)**: Razer Naga, Logitech G600, Corsair Scimitar and other mice whose side buttons send keyboard keys.
5. **[How it works](how-it-works.md)**: what happens inside the game when you hold the button, and why some things are not possible.
6. **[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)**: answers to common questions and problems.

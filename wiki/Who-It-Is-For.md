# Who it is for

MouseShift is for anyone who wants more keybinds within easy reach without stretching the hand. Some players benefit more than others.

## Healers

Healing asks more of the keyboard than any other role: heals, cooldowns, dispels, utility, movement abilities and often some damage, many of them cast on a specific player.

With mouseover healing you point at a raid frame and press a key, and the spell lands on that player without changing your target. MouseShift adds layers to those keys: point at the frame, hold the thumb button, press 2, and the Shift-2 heal goes to that player. Your mouseover macros need no change.

If you heal with click-casting (Clique, Grid2, VuhDo, Cell or the game's click bindings), keep your clicks as they are. MouseShift adds layers to your keys, so clicks and keys together cover more spells than either alone.

## Damage dealers and tanks

Rotations, cooldowns, defensives, interrupts, crowd control, utility, consumables: modern classes have more buttons than easy keys. MouseShift puts the second and third versions of your keys under the thumb, so important abilities no longer end up on keys you have to search for.

## Owners of MMO mice

Mice such as the Razer Naga, Logitech G600 or Corsair Scimitar have a grid of side buttons that usually send keyboard keys. MouseShift can turn one of them into Shift, Ctrl or Alt without the mouse software running, or give the whole grid extra layers from another button.

## Players who find modifier keys uncomfortable

Long sessions, hand fatigue, limited reach or simply a small hand make the little finger on Shift, Ctrl and Alt tiring. Moving the modifier to the thumb, the strongest finger, keeps the same keybinds with much less strain.

## Players who used AutoHotKey or mouse software for this

If you already made a mouse button act as Shift outside the game, MouseShift gives you the same inside the game: no extra program, the same on every computer, and settings that travel with your addons.

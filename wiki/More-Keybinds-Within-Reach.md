# More keybinds within reach

Say your left hand reaches 12 keys comfortably while moving: 1 to 6, Q, E, R, F, Z and X.

| Modifiers on the mouse | Layers you reach | Keybinds within reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None | plain | 12 |
| Shift | plain, Shift | 24 (double) |
| Shift and Ctrl | plain, Shift, Ctrl | 36 (triple) |
| Shift, Ctrl and Alt | plain, Shift, Ctrl, Alt | 48 (quadruple) |
| Shift, Ctrl and Alt, holding two or three together | plus Ctrl-Shift, Alt-Shift, Alt-Ctrl, Alt-Ctrl-Shift | up to 96 |

All of it without the left hand leaving its usual keys. Most players use one or two buttons, which already doubles or triples what they reach.

## Ways to organise the layers

**One layer per purpose.** Plain keys for the rotation, Shift for cooldowns and defensives, Ctrl for utility and crowd control.

**Stronger versions on the same key.** 1 is a fast heal, Shift-1 a big heal on the same key. Related spells share a key and the thumb picks the version.

**Action bars as layers.** Action bar 1 on 1 to 6, action bar 2 on Shift-1 to Shift-6, action bar 3 on Ctrl-1 to Ctrl-6. Each bar is one layer of the same keys, which makes the setup easy to remember.

## An example for a healer

Thumb button as Shift, the next side button as Ctrl, and 8 keys under the left hand:

| Key | Plain | Shift (thumb) | Ctrl (second button) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fast heal | Big heal | Group heal |
| 2 | Heal over time | Shield | Second heal over time |
| 3 | Dispel | Mass dispel | Purge on enemy |
| 4 | Damage spell | Second damage spell | Interrupt |
| Q | Cooldown on target | Personal defensive | Movement ability |
| E | Group cooldown | Second group cooldown | Battle resurrection |
| R | Resurrect | Mass resurrect | Mount |
| F | Trinket | Second trinket | Healthstone |

24 actions on 8 keys, all reachable without moving the hand.
